# SES S.A. Valuation Model | DCF & Comparable Company Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is a corporate finance and investment analysis case study focused on the valuation of **SES S.A. (SESG)**, a Luxembourg-headquartered satellite and connectivity operator listed on Euronext Paris and the Luxembourg Stock Exchange.

The objective was to build a professional valuation model that combines:

- Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) valuation
- Comparable Company Analysis (CCA)
- Five-year operating forecasts
- WACC estimation
- Sensitivity analysis
- Football-field valuation reconciliation
- Analyst-style investment memo

The project was developed as an educational portfolio exercise designed to demonstrate financial modelling, valuation methodology, analytical reasoning, documentation discipline, and investment communication.

---

## Business Question

> Based on publicly available financial information, what is a reasonable fair value range for SES S.A., and what factors are likely to drive future shareholder value creation or destruction?

The analysis focuses on:

- Revenue growth sustainability
- EBITDA margin recovery
- Cash flow generation
- Capital intensity
- Leverage and deleveraging potential
- Valuation sensitivity to market assumptions
- Relative valuation versus listed peers

## Methodology

### 1. Historical Analysis

Historical financial data was collected from official SES investor-relations materials and used to analyse:

- Revenue trends
- EBITDA trends
- Margin development
- Cash generation
- Leverage evolution

Special attention was given to the impact of the Intelsat acquisition on FY2025 comparability.

---

### 2. Operating Forecast

A five-year operating forecast (FY2026E–FY2030E) was constructed using key value drivers:

- Revenue growth
- EBITDA margin
- Depreciation and amortisation
- Capital expenditure
- Working capital requirements
- Corporate taxation

Three scenarios were modelled:

- Base Case
- Upside Case
- Downside Case

---

### 3. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)

The DCF valuation uses:

- Unlevered Free Cash Flow (FCFF)
- Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)
- Gordon Growth terminal value methodology

Enterprise Value is calculated as:

FCFF Present Value + Terminal Value Present Value

An equity value bridge then adjusts for:

- Net debt
- Minority interests

to derive indicative shareholder value.

---

### 4. Comparable Company Analysis (CCA)

A peer group consisting of satellite and connectivity operators was used to construct valuation benchmarks based on:

- EV / EBITDA
- P/E ratios (where meaningful)

Peer companies include:

- Eutelsat Communications
- Iridium Communications
- Viasat
- Telesat
- EchoStar

Comparable-company valuation serves as a market-based cross-check against the intrinsic DCF valuation.

---

### 5. Football-Field Valuation

A football-field analysis reconciles:

- DCF Valuation Range
- Comparable Company Range

The final valuation range uses:

- 60% DCF weighting
- 40% Comparable Company weighting

This weighting reflects the importance of long-term cash-flow generation while retaining a market-based valuation cross-check.

## Key Valuation Drivers

The most influential drivers within the model are:

- Revenue growth assumptions
- EBITDA margin recovery
- Capital expenditure intensity
- Cost of capital (WACC)
- Terminal growth rate
- Net debt reduction
- Intelsat integration outcomes

Sensitivity testing demonstrates that enterprise value remains highly responsive to changes in discount-rate and terminal-growth assumptions.

## Limitations

This project should be interpreted as an analytical portfolio exercise rather than investment advice.

Important limitations include:

- Future operating performance may differ materially from forecasts.
- Peer multiples are market snapshots and change over time.
- The valuation is sensitive to WACC and terminal-growth assumptions.
- Forecasts require judgement regarding post-Intelsat integration outcomes.
- Market-sensitive inputs should be refreshed before any real-world investment application.

The valuation should therefore be interpreted as a range of potential outcomes rather than a precise estimate of intrinsic value.
